## What this is

Most students can name about seven careers. The world of work has hundreds, and many of the best-paid ones did not exist when their parents chose a stream. The new [Career Roadmaps](/roadmaps/) page on this site takes 26 of today's roles and answers three questions for each one:

1. **Which school subjects keep this door open?** This matters most in Grade 10, before AS Level or Class 11 choices.
2. **Which Indian degrees and entrance exams lead there?** Most of these jobs do not need one specific degree, but some routes make the start much easier.
3. **What do you learn first, next, and on the job?** Three phases, plus a small project you can start now.

The roles cover seven families: software engineering, data and AI, cloud and security, product and design, business, marketing and sales, finance and consulting, and media. You can filter the page by a subject you are keeping, by an entrance exam, or by family, and send a classmate a link straight to one role.

## The one rule to remember

**The biggest door-closer is dropping Mathematics after IGCSE or Class 10.** It shuts most routes into engineering, data, AI, finance and consulting, in India and abroad. If you are unsure, keeping Maths to AS Level or Class 12 is the safer choice. A student who keeps Maths and later chooses design or media has lost nothing. A student who drops it and later wants data science has a hard climb.

## How to use it on Subject Choices Day

- Pick three roles that sound interesting, even vaguely. Open each one on the Roadmaps page.
- Write down the subjects each role wants. If a subject appears in all three, that is your answer.
- Look at the entrance exams. Some, like UCEED for design, accept any stream. Others, like JEE, need Physics, Chemistry and Maths.
- Bring the list to your FP counsellor. They can tell you which routes fit your marks and your family's plans.

## A note for Cambridge students

Most Indian entrance exams and universities accept A Level, but some ask for a UGC equivalence certificate (formerly issued by the AIU) or a minimum mark in specific subjects. Check this early, not in Grade 12. The FP team arranges the certificate for students who need it.
