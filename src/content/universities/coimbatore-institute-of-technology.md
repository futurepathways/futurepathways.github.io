## At a glance

- **Type:** A government-aided autonomous college (it sets its own syllabus and exams) affiliated to Anna University, Chennai. It was founded in 1956. Some seats are aided, with a low fee. Others are self-supporting (SF), with a higher fee.
- **Location:** Civil Aerodrome Post, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.
- **Accreditation:** NAAC grade A, with a score (CGPA) of 3.09, after the second-cycle visit in May 2025. NAAC is the national body that grades colleges on quality.
- **Ranking:** In the 101 to 150 band for Engineering in NIRF 2025, the government's own ranking. NIRF 2026 was not on the official site when this was checked.
- **How you get in:** Only through TNEA, the Tamil Nadu government's counselling on Class 12 marks. There is no entrance exam for B.E. or B.Tech.

## Popular courses

For 2026-27, AICTE approved 180 seats in Computer Science and Engineering, and 120 each in Electronics and Communication, Electrical and Electronics, and Mechanical. Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, Information Technology, Chemical and Civil have 60 each. A new B.E. in Electronics Engineering (VLSI Design and Technology) starts in 2026-27 with 60 seats. (VLSI is the design of computer chips.)

## How to get in

### For CBSE and other Indian board students

1. **Eligibility.** A pass in Class 12 with Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry, with an average of at least 45 percent for OC, or 40 percent for BC, MBC, DNC, SC and ST students.
2. **Nativity.** A student who studied Classes 8 to 12 in Tamil Nadu needs no nativity certificate. A Tamil Nadu native who studied outside the state needs an e-Nativity Certificate.
3. **Your rank.** TNEA builds a score out of 200 from Mathematics (100), Physics (50) and Chemistry (50). CBSE and ISC marks are normalised (adjusted) against the State Board using the top national mark in each subject.
4. **Choice filling.** Online counselling by rank. CIT has both aided and self-supporting seats, at different fees, so check which one you are choosing.

### For Cambridge AS and A Level students

1. **Eligibility.** The same 45 or 40 percent rule, on A Level Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry. CIT's own page says an equivalent qualification must be one approved by the Tamil Nadu boards. TNEA treats Cambridge as an "Other Board".
2. **Marks, not just grades.** TNEA says a student with grade certificates must also show actual marks. Otherwise only the lowest mark of each grade band counts.
3. **Normalisation.** TNEA asks "Other Boards" for their top mark in each subject. If it is not available in time, the board's top mark is treated as 100 percent.
4. **Equivalence.** CIT's site does not mention an equivalence certificate. Equivalence for foreign qualifications is now issued by the University Grants Commission (UGC) through its equivalence portal. Ask the college early whether it needs one.
5. **Timing.** Cambridge March-series results came out on 19 May 2026, before TNEA counselling in July. Keep every subject in the March series.

## Application window and fee

For the 2026 cycle, TNEA registration ran from 3 May to 5 June 2026, the rank list came on 1 July, and general counselling ran from 20 July to 30 August. The fee was ₹500, or ₹250 for SC, SCA and ST students. There is no separate CIT application for B.E. or B.Tech. The 2027 dates were not out when this was checked.

## Approximate annual cost

CIT's own figures for the first year, 2026-27:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Aided seat, tuition and college fee | ₹33,350 |
| Self-supporting seat (SF), in two terms | ₹92,000 (₹56,000 + ₹36,000) |
| Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (SF), in two terms | ₹87,000 (₹53,000 + ₹34,000) |
| Boys' hostel, double room, room and mess | ₹1,00,000 |
| Boys' hostel, single room, room and mess | ₹1,25,000 |
| Girls' hostel, first year, room and mess | about ₹90,000 |
| Hostel caution deposit, refundable, paid once | ₹10,000 |

So a family should budget about **₹1.3 lakh (aided seat) to ₹2.2 lakh (SF seat, single room) for the first year in 2026-27**, before books and travel. SF fees have risen a little each year. Hostel fees are paid by UPI or bank transfer.

## Scholarships

- **Government schemes through TNEA:** 7.5 percent seats for government school students (fees paid by the state), the First Graduate concession, and the AICTE Tuition Fee Waiver on self-supporting seats for families earning under ₹8 lakh.
- **State and central scholarships** listed by CIT, with income limits. For example, the BC, MBC and DNC scholarship and the SC and ST post-matric scholarship need family income below ₹2.5 lakh. The Central Sector Scholarship needs 80 percent marks and income below ₹8 lakh. AICTE Pragati for girls needs income below ₹8 lakh.
- **Full fee concession** for SC and ST students on aided courses, under Tamil Nadu rules.
- **Alumni and endowment funds:** CIT lists about a dozen funds set up by former batches. It does not publish the amounts or criteria, so ask the Principal's office.

## NRI, OCI and foreign-national routes

There is no separate route. AICTE's approval for 2026-27 shows "No" for NRI and for international students in every undergraduate branch. TNEA lets OCI or PIO cardholders who are Tamil Nadu natives apply under the Open Category, unless they were born abroad after 4 March 2021.

## Things families should know

- **Aided seats are among the cheapest good engineering seats in Tamil Nadu.** But the same branch can also have SF seats at almost three times the tuition. Check the seat type before you lock a choice.
- **No entrance exam.** Class 12 marks decide the rank, and Mathematics counts for half.
- **A management quota exists, but not through the college.** CIT's 2026-27 disclosure shows some management-quota admissions, mostly on self-supporting seats. These are filled through the Consortium of Self Financing Professional, Arts and Science Colleges in Tamil Nadu. The fee for these seats is not published. Ask before you plan on it.
- **Study abroad.** We found no "2+2" degree with a university abroad on CIT's site. Ask the college's international office what exchanges run now.
