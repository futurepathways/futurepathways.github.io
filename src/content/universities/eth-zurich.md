## ETH Zurich Admission, Two Honest Routes for Indian Students

Please read this part with care. ETH is not like the other universities in this series. An Indian Class 12 certificate (CBSE, CISCE or a State Board) or a Cambridge A Level result does not, by itself, give a place on a Bachelor's course. ETH's own rules for India have two cases. If a student holds a Class 12 certificate and a place at a recognised Indian university in the same subject, ETH asks for a reduced entrance examination. Otherwise it asks for a comprehensive one. The examination is in German. So the table below is built by route, not by board. For most Indian families the Master's route, after an Indian Bachelor's degree, is the sensible plan. The Bachelor's route is only realistic for a child who starts German early.

We could not confirm that Cambridge International A Levels are treated the same way, so families must check ETH's own country page. | Students who finish a strong Bachelor's degree first, then apply to an English taught ETH Master's. > **My planning angle:** If your child is in Grade 8 or 9 and dreams of Europe, start German now. Reaching C1 usually takes several years of steady study, so ask a recognised language institute for a realistic plan. That keeps both ETH routes open. If your child is already in Grade 11 or 12, the wise plan is a strong Bachelor's degree first, with good marks and some research or project work. Then apply to an English taught ETH Master's in November of the final year. Whichever route you choose, check ETH's own country page for India before you pay for any test or exam.

## Why Should Your Child Consider ETH Zurich?

### Einstein's University, Top 10 in the World

ETH Zurich is joint number 8 in the QS World University Rankings 2027, level with UCL. ETH says this is its 12th year in a row in the QS top 10, and that it is the highest ranked university in continental Europe. Its QS score is 96.3. Albert Einstein studied at ETH and later taught there. For students of science and engineering, ETH is one of the strongest places to study in Europe.

### The Cost Picture

Tuition is low for a university of this standing. Foreign students pay CHF 2,190 per semester, or CHF 4,380 a year (~INR 5.1 lakhs at ~CHF 116/INR, October 2026). Switzerland tripled this fee for foreign students from autumn 2025, and it is still modest. A full 2 year Master's is about CHF 8,760 in tuition (~INR 10 lakhs). Zurich itself is expensive. ETH's own sheet puts living costs at CHF 22,100 a year (~INR 26 lakhs). Together that is about CHF 26,480 a year (~INR 31 lakhs). The Swiss visa also needs proof of CHF 21,000 a year, kept in the student's own Swiss bank account. For top Master's students, ETH's ESOP award may cover tuition and give a living grant, but check the current amount on ETH's page.

### The Language Reality, Why We Present ETH Honestly

Every other university in this series teaches undergraduates in English. ETH does not. Bachelor's programmes are taught mainly in German. A C1 certificate is needed by 31 March, and the entrance examination for Indian certificate holders is also in German. We say this plainly because families deserve honest advice. The same fact opens two real options. First, a child who starts German in Grade 8 or 9 can reach the Bachelor's route, and German also opens other German speaking universities. Second, the English taught Master's route needs no German for most programmes and no entrance exam. The application window is only one month, in November. ETH is not the easiest door in this series, but it is a rewarding one for a family that plans early.

### Switzerland and the Indian Student Community

ETH runs an Ambassadors blog where current students, including Indian students, write about daily life in Zurich. Reading a few posts is a good way for a child to picture the move. We have not re-checked Indian student groups in Zurich this time, so please ask me before you rely on one. Parents should also know that Swiss visa and bank rules are strict, and that the family must plan the funds early. I can help you build that plan.

## What parents should know

- CRITICAL. The 2027 Bachelor's start is closed for anyone who must sit ETH's entrance exam (the application deadline was 31 March 2026, and ETH says a student who passes the exam can only enter in the following autumn). For a child now in Class 12, the earliest Bachelor's start is autumn 2028. The Bachelor's route needs a German C1 certificate by 31 March, and the entrance exam is in German in January. ETH is also changing its Bachelor's entry rules from autumn 2028, with details from spring 2027.
- CRITICAL. The Swiss visa needs proof of CHF 21,000 a year (~INR 24 lakhs). It must sit in the STUDENT'S OWN Swiss bank account, or come as a guarantee from a Swiss resident. A parents' support letter is not accepted. Plan the transfer early.
- CRITICAL. Class 12 or A Level results do not give direct Bachelor's entry. ETH lists exam free entry only for certain grades and subjects, and for Indian certificates it asks for a reduced or comprehensive entrance exam. Check ETH's own country page.
- Recommended. Master's route: English taught, one application window from 1 to 30 November (30 November 2026 for autumn 2027 entry), no entrance exam. Programmes set their own English score and some ask for the GRE.
- Tuition. CHF 2,190 per semester for foreign students (CHF 4,380 a year, ~INR 5.1 lakhs at ~CHF 116/INR, October 2026). A 2 year Master's costs about CHF 8,760 (~INR 10 lakhs) in tuition.
- Living costs. ETH's own figure is CHF 22,100 a year (~INR 26 lakhs). With tuition, about CHF 26,480 a year (~INR 31 lakhs).
- ESOP. An award for Master's students that earlier ETH pages said covers tuition and gives a living grant of CHF 12,000 a semester (~INR 14 lakhs). Check the amount, the deadline and whether a thesis outline is needed on ETH's page.
- Entrance exam fee. CHF 550 for the reduced exam and CHF 800 for the comprehensive exam. The exam is in German.
- Fee and one choice. The Bachelor's application is for one programme only. Confirm the application fee in eApply.
