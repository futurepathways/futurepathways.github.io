## At a glance

Yale Campus: New Haven, Connecticut Watch Yale’s own admissions video on YouTube to hear students say why they chose Yale. Ranked #16 Globally (QS 2027)* *QS World University Rankings 2027, published in June 2026. In the Times Higher Education World University Rankings 2027, published on 30 September 2026, Yale shares 9th place. Different rankings use different methods, so always read the name and the year.

- **University:** Yale University · Member of the Ivy League · 14 residential colleges · More than 80 majors for undergraduates
- **Location:** New Haven, Connecticut, USA · About 2 hours from New York City · A long journey from India, so plan flights well ahead
- **Founded:** 1701 · About 6,700 undergraduates and about 15,700 students in all · About 24% of all students are international
- **Legacy:** 5 US Presidents studied here (Taft, Ford, GHW Bush, Clinton, GW Bush) · 72 Nobel Prize winners linked to Yale (as of October 2025)
- **Curriculum Accepted:** CBSE, ICSE, State Boards, IB and Cambridge A Level (no fixed percentage cut off)

You can see Yale on video at any time. [Watch “That’s Why I Chose Yale” here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=64ujJviaPzQ). This is Yale’s own admissions video on YouTube. Yale’s admissions office also lists student led campus tours and virtual tour options on admissions.yale.edu.

## Yale Admission Requirements — CBSE/State Boards and Cambridge A Level

| Requirement | CBSE / Indian State Boards | Cambridge A Level |
|---|---|---|
| Boards Accepted | CBSE, ICSE, State Boards and IB are all accepted. Yale reads each student in the context of their own school. It publishes no fixed percentage cut off. Apply in Class 12 with your Class 11 marks and predicted results. Board results come in about May, after the application is decided. | Cambridge AS and A Level are accepted. Your school sends predicted grades with the application. Final results (in May) are checked after you are admitted. |
| Standardised Testing — SAT or ACT Required | SAT or ACT is required for every first year applicant, with no exception for international students. Yale says AP or IB scores cannot take the place of the SAT or ACT, but you may send them as well. Indian board exams do not count as a substitute. Check Yale’s page for any score guidance. | Same rule. A Level results do not replace the SAT or ACT. Plan to sit the SAT or ACT well before the application deadline. |
| English Proficiency | Needed if you have not studied in English medium for at least two years. Yale accepts TOEFL, IELTS, Cambridge English Qualifications, Duolingo English Test and InitialView. Most CBSE English medium students will not need it, but check. | Usually not needed if your A Level school teaches in English. Check Yale’s page if you are unsure. |
| Essays | A main essay on the Common Application (or the Coalition Application) plus short Yale questions. Yale wants a real voice, not a polished one. Everything must be in English. Yale does not accept self translated documents; ask for a certified translation. | Same essays and same guidance for all applicants. |
| Interview | Optional and by invitation only. You cannot ask for one. Yale says many successful applicants are not interviewed. Interviews can be held online. Not being invited is not a bad sign. | Same for all applicants. |
| Application Portal | Common Application, Coalition Application (on Scoir) or QuestBridge. You may use only one. Yale says it gives no advantage to any of the three. Yale does not use UCAS. | Same three options for all applicants. |
| Single-Choice Early Action (SCEA) | Deadline: 1 November 2026. Non binding, so you can still compare offers. It is restrictive: you cannot also apply to other private universities under their early plans. Read the exact rule on Yale’s page before you choose. Yale says SCEA gives no advantage in itself. | Same rule and same deadline. Your school must send predicted grades in time. |
| Regular Decision (RD) | Deadline: 2 January 2027. No limit on other applications. Check Yale’s page for the decision date. | Same deadline for all applicants. |
| Financial Aid for International Students — CRITICAL | Yale says a family’s ability to pay is not a factor in admission “for any student, anywhere in the world”. Aid is worked out after the offer. Yale looks at the gap between the US economy and your home country’s economy. Aid forms have their own deadlines, separate from the application. Check Yale’s financial aid page before you register. | Same policy for all international applicants. |
| If Admitted With Need | Yale says it meets 100% of proven need with scholarships, not loans. Yale says families with income below USD 100,000 (about ~INR 96 lakhs) and typical assets pay nothing. Families below USD 200,000 get tuition covered. These limits began for students entering in 2026-27. | Same policy for all admitted international students. |
| Visa | F-1 student visa. Yale gives you the I-20 form after admission. You must list your social media accounts from the past five years and set them to public before the visa interview. Interview wait times change by city and by month. Check the US State Department page. India is not on the US travel ban list. | Same process for all international students. |

## Common to both pathways

Yale reads every applicant in the context of their own school, so there is no fixed grade cut off. Every applicant must send the SAT or ACT, teacher recommendation letters and a school report with the transcript. AP or IB scores can be added, but they cannot replace the SAT or ACT. Many older guides still call Yale “test flexible”. That is out of date.

## Why Should Your Child Consider Yale?

### A Top 20 University With Strong Humanities and Law

Yale is 16th in the QS World University Rankings 2027 and shares 9th place in the Times Higher Education World University Rankings 2027. Both lists came out this year. 72 Nobel Prize winners have been linked to Yale, and 5 US Presidents studied there: Taft, Ford, George H.W. Bush, Clinton and George W. Bush. Yale is especially known for law, history, political science, drama and the arts. Many universities in this series lean towards engineering and science. Yale suits a student who loves ideas and argument as much as equations. Yale also has strong science and computing, so it is not a narrow choice.

### Need-Blind and Full Need Met, Including for Indian Families

Yale says its admissions office does not consider whether a family can pay, “for any student, anywhere in the world”. Only a handful of US universities say this for international students and also meet full need. Yale’s own pages say it meets 100% of proven need with scholarships and without requiring loans. For students entering in 2026-27, families with income below USD 100,000 (about ~INR 96 lakhs at ~USD 96/INR, October 2026) and typical assets pay nothing. Families below USD 200,000 (about ~INR 1.9 crores) have tuition covered. Yale says its average yearly need-based scholarship is USD 75,220 (about ~INR 72 lakhs), and 88% of graduates leave with no student loan debt. The full cost of attendance for 2026-27 is USD 97,985 (about ~INR 94 lakhs). Families who receive aid do not pay that full amount. Please read the cost note in the section below. Yale says its aid for international students allows for housing, meals and flights home, and adjusts for the difference between the US economy and your home country.

### The Residential Colleges

Every Yale undergraduate belongs to one of 14 residential colleges. Each is a smaller community inside the large university. It has its own dining hall, courtyard and traditions. A student is never just one face among about 6,700 undergraduates. Yale’s libraries and collections hold about 15.9 million volumes, so the resources are those of a very large research university. For a student who wants a close community and big resources together, this is one of Yale’s best features.

### Yale and India

About 24% of Yale’s students are international, so a student from India will not be alone. Yale’s International Students and Scholars office supports new arrivals, and Indian students are active across departments. After the degree, F-1 students can work in the US through OPT (Optional Practical Training). STEM graduates get a longer period than others. The US Department of Homeland Security says it is reviewing OPT, but we found no proposed rule that ends or shortens it. A new fee for OPT applications has been proposed. Check the latest position when your child applies. Yale’s alumni network is strong in law, government, teaching, journalism and the arts.

## What parents should know

- CRITICAL: Yale now requires the SAT or ACT from every first year applicant, including international students. AP, IB, A Level and Indian board results cannot replace it. Many guides still say Yale is “test flexible”, and that is wrong. Book SAT or ACT dates early, so the scores arrive before 1 November (SCEA) or 2 January (RD)
- Deadlines for 2027 entry: Single-Choice Early Action on 1 November 2026 and Regular Decision on 2 January 2027. Financial aid forms for international applicants have their own dates, so read Yale’s financial aid page when you register. CBSE and Cambridge A Level results both come in May, so Yale decides on Class 11 marks and predicted grades
- Cost: the full cost of attendance for 2026-27 is USD 97,985 (about ~INR 94 lakhs at ~USD 96/INR, October 2026). It includes tuition of USD 72,500, housing of USD 12,080 and food of USD 9,520. The rupee has become weaker, so the rupee cost has risen even where the dollar cost looks steady. Most aid families pay far less than this
- Aid: Yale says it meets 100% of proven need with scholarships and without requiring loans. Families with income below USD 100,000 and typical assets pay nothing. Yale does not say clearly how these income limits work for families whose income is in rupees, so the aid office decides each case after admission. “Meets full need” does not always mean free. Ask for a personal estimate from Yale’s cost calculators before you decide
- Competition is very high. For the Class of 2030, Yale admitted 2,328 students from 54,919 applicants, about 4.2%. International students have no special route, and a strong application still may not succeed
- The alumni interview is by invitation only. You cannot request one, and not being invited does not harm an application
- Student visa: F-1 applicants must list social media accounts from the past five years and set them to public before the interview. Visa and SEVIS fees apply, and a new visa integrity fee may be added. Check the State Department page for the current amounts. India is not on the US travel ban list
- New rule from 15 September 2026: the US has ended “duration of status” for F-1 students. A student is now admitted for a fixed period tied to the programme, with 4 years as the most. If the degree takes longer, the student must apply to extend. The time allowed to leave after the course is reported to fall from 60 to 30 days. The FP team will ask Yale’s international office how this affects your child
- Work after graduation: the USD 100,000 H-1B employer fee is currently blocked by US courts, but the rules are changing fast. Do not plan a child’s career around any one US visa rule. Check again before the application and before the visa interview
