## At a glance

- **What it is:** A private university set up by the Government of Haryana on 13 February 2009, as the state's first private university. The Ministry of Education made it an Institution of Eminence on 4 November 2020. An Institution of Eminence is a status given to a small number of universities, with more freedom over courses and fees. JGU now styles itself an "Institution of Eminence Deemed to be University" (as on 18 Oct 2026).
- **Where:** Sonipat, Haryana, on Sonipat Narela Road, near the Delhi border.
- **Quality marks:** In the QS World University Rankings by Subject 2026, JGU says it is 35th in the world and first in India for Law, and 90th in the world for Politics and International Studies. It does not appear in the NIRF 2025 university, overall or law lists. We could not find a current NAAC grade on its website.
- **Many schools in one:** Law, business, international affairs, liberal arts, and more. Each school sets its own rules, so always read the page for your exact programme.

## Which courses are most popular?

JGU is best known for its law degrees (B.A. LL.B., B.B.A. LL.B. and B.Com. LL.B., all five-year honours degrees), BBA (Hons), and B.A. (Hons) Global Affairs. It also offers three-year B.A. degrees in Legal Studies, Criminology and Criminal Justice, and AI and Law, plus liberal arts and other programmes.

## How do CBSE and other Indian board students get in?

- **Five-year law (B.A. LL.B. and others):** Class 12 with at least 45 percent, plus the LNAT-UK test. LNAT (Law National Aptitude Test) is a UK law admissions test run with Pearson VUE, taken on computer at test centres in India and abroad. JGU says it accepts no other law admissions test, so CLAT is not accepted for these degrees. Admission is based on the LNAT score.
- **BBA (Hons):** A Class 12 pass. Tests and cut-offs: SAT 1100 or above, ACT 27 or above, CUET at the 60th percentile or above, or JSAT (JGU's own online test) 50 percent or above. Selection has three rounds: an application review (marks, two short written answers, test score, activities), JSAT, and an online faculty interview.
- **B.A. (Hons) Global Affairs:** A Class 12 pass, a JSAT, SAT or ACT score, a personal statement, a supplemental question and a faculty interview.
- **Three-year B.A. courses in law:** The law admissions portal lists them under LNAT-UK for 2027. Some programme pages also list CLAT or CUET, so confirm with JGU which tests count.

## How do Cambridge AS & A Level students get in?

- **Law:** Cambridge is a listed board. The same 45 percent rule and the LNAT-UK test apply.
- **BBA (Hons):** Grade 10 equivalent (IGCSE) with at least five subjects including English, plus two or three subjects at A Level. No minimum grade is stated.
- **Global Affairs:** Cambridge is a listed board; no minimum grade is stated.
- **Grade conversion:** JGU does not publish one. Ask how they will check the 45 percent rule for law.
- **Equivalence certificate:** The Business School page asks for a board "recognised by AIU". Since April 2025, equivalence for foreign school qualifications is granted by the UGC through its equivalence portal, so ask JGU admissions whether they need a certificate from a Cambridge student.
- **A plus for Cambridge students:** LNAT is already familiar to students who apply for law in the UK, so one test can serve both.

## When do applications open for 2027 entry, and what is the fee?

- **Law:** The law admissions portal says admissions are open for 2027. LNAT testing for 2027 entry began on 1 September 2026. JGU fills law seats in an early and a later phase. Press reports of the JGLS announcement (third-party, as on 18 Oct 2026) give the phases as: early phase, take the LNAT between 1 September and 30 November 2026, with at least half the seats filled here; late phase, take it between 1 December 2026 and 28 February 2027. A second LNAT attempt is allowed in the next phase. Confirm these dates on the portal and book an LNAT slot early.
- **Other schools:** The programme pages did not show 2027 deadlines on 15 November 2026.
- **Fees to apply:** JGU's public pages do not show the application fee; it appears in the portal. LNAT has its own test fee, paid to LNAT. After a BBA offer, a registration payment of ₹1,25,000 secures the seat.

## How much does it cost a year?

From JGU's programme pages (the year is not printed on the pages; read as the current cycle):

| Programme | Tuition | Hostel | Food and services | Total a year |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B.A. LL.B. (Hons) | about ₹7.5 lakh | about ₹2.32 lakh | about ₹1.53 lakh | about ₹11.35 lakh |
| BBA (Hons) | about ₹5 lakh | about ₹2.31 lakh | about ₹1.25 lakh | about ₹8.56 lakh |
| B.A. (Hons) Global Affairs | about ₹4.5 lakh | about ₹2.32 lakh | about ₹1.53 lakh | about ₹8.35 lakh |

"Food and services" covers food, laundry, security, housekeeping and electricity. Add a one-time refundable deposit of ₹50,000. All charges rise by 10 percent every year, so a five-year law degree costs well over ₹60 lakh before scholarships.

## Are there scholarships?

- **Merit-cum-means:** Based on marks, test score, interview, family income and activities. They cover tuition only, never hostel or food, are limited, and go first come, first served. You cannot combine two awards.
- **Law:** Merit-cum-means scholarships for students who qualify through LNAT-UK, subject to parental income tax return rules.
- **Business School:** The undergraduate application page offers a 20 to 35 percent tuition waiver for students with 80 percent or more in Class 12 (as on 18 Oct 2026). Ask JGU how Cambridge marks are read against the 80 percent rule. Scholarship amounts are adjusted against the second-semester fee, and the rules are reviewed every year.
- **Loans:** JGU lists education loan tie-ups with Avanse, Axis Bank, Credila and others.

## Can NRI, OCI and foreign students apply?

JGU states that it has no NRI or foreign national quota, no management quota and no donation seats. Everyone goes through the same merit process, with fees in rupees. Foreign nationals need an Indian student visa and must register on the e-FRRO portal within 14 days of arriving.

## What should families know before applying?

- **CLAT does not get you into JGU's five-year law degrees.** Only LNAT-UK counts. Many families still assume CLAT works.
- **Book LNAT early.** Seats are filled in phases, starting with students who test early.
- **Plan the money.** Fees rise 10 percent every year, and scholarships cover tuition only.
- **Read the page for your programme.** Rules, tests and minimum marks differ between JGU's schools.
- **No quotas, no donations.** Apply only through jgu.edu.in and its portals, and report anyone who asks for money for a seat.
