Caltech Campus, Pasadena, California A 124-acre campus near Los Angeles. See it through the virtual tour and the visit page on the Caltech admissions website. Ranked #7 Globally (QS World University Rankings 2027) Also #7 in the THE World University Rankings 2027 · The smallest university in this series, with fewer than 1,000 undergraduates.

- **University:** California Institute of Technology (Caltech) · 6 academic divisions · Science and engineering focus, with a required core in humanities and social science too
- **Location:** Pasadena, California, USA · 124-acre campus near Los Angeles · A long journey from India with at least one connecting flight
- **Founded:** 1891 (as Throop University; renamed Caltech in 1920) · 971 undergraduates and 1,398 graduate students · Student-faculty ratio 3:1
- **Legacy:** 48 Nobel laureates (49 Nobel Prizes) among faculty and alumni, as counted by Caltech
- **Signature Asset:** Caltech manages NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL), which sends probes to explore the solar system

## Is Caltech Right for Your Child? Read This First

Caltech is a very small and very demanding place. Its core course asks every student to study basic sciences, humanities and social science, whatever the major. Caltech lists 33 undergraduate majors, mostly in science and engineering. This suits a child who enjoys long, hard problem sets and would rather go deep into one question than wide across many subjects. It may not suit a child who is good at many subjects but does not love maths and physics. It may also not suit a child who wants a large campus with a lot of variety. The numbers also say it is a long shot. For the class of 2029, Caltech had 11,285 applicants and a first-year class of 248. Fit matters more here than almost anywhere, so please read this edition with that in mind.

## Common to both pathways

Every applicant uses the Common App or QuestBridge, takes the SAT or ACT, and sends two teacher letters. Caltech reads each application in the context of the applicant’s own school and country. The strongest signal is depth in maths, with at least one year each of physics and chemistry behind it. Caltech’s pages do not list JEE or NEET rank as a requirement.

## Voices from Caltech, Alumni Who Shaped Science

Caltech’s own list of Nobel laureates includes Kip Thorne (BS 1962), who won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2017. It also includes Andrea Ghez (MS 1989, PhD 1993), who won the Physics prize in 2020. Ardem Patapoutian (PhD 1996) won the Prize in Physiology or Medicine in 2021. Caltech’s list covers faculty, alumni, trustees and current and former postdocs.

## Why Should Your Child Consider Caltech?

### QS #7 and THE #7, With a Nobel Record Few Can Match

Caltech is #7 in the QS World University Rankings 2027 and #7 in the THE World University Rankings 2027. Rankings move from year to year, so treat them as a rough guide. Caltech says its faculty and alumni include 48 Nobel laureates, who share 49 prizes. It has only 971 undergraduates, and the student-faculty ratio is 3:1. That means undergraduates can work close to the researchers who do this work. Caltech also manages NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL), which sends probes to explore the planets of our solar system.

### A Common Core and Early Research

Every Caltech undergraduate takes the same core, which covers basic sciences, humanities and social science, whatever the major. Caltech has six academic divisions and 33 undergraduate majors. Students find research early. Most undergraduates join the Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowship (SURF), a 10 week paid programme where students work with faculty on a research proposal. Student life is organised around nine residential communities. An honor system shapes how students behave and work. Caltech says its campus has about one PhD researcher for every student.

### Financial Aid, Generous But With One Strict Rule

Caltech is need aware for international students. This means the amount a family can pay may count in the admission decision. For US citizens it is need blind. If your child is admitted, Caltech meets 100 percent of the demonstrated need. The average grant for the class entering in fall 2026 was just under USD 69,000 (~INR 66 lakhs at ~USD 96/INR, October 2026).

Please plan around one strict rule. An international student must ask for aid when they apply. A student who does not apply for aid, or applies but does not qualify in the first year, cannot apply again in any later year. Caltech gives no merit scholarships. All aid is based on need. The total cost for 2026-27 on campus is USD 98,622 a year (~INR 95 lakhs at ~USD 96/INR, October 2026). Of this, USD 93,225 is billed by Caltech for tuition, fees, housing and meals. The rest is an estimate for books and personal costs.

### Caltech and India

Caltech does not publish a list of accepted Indian boards that we could find. CBSE, State Board and Cambridge A Level students all apply through the same Common App or QuestBridge route. Caltech’s requirement pages ask for the SAT or ACT, two teacher letters, essays and English proof if your school does not teach in English. They do not list JEE or NEET.

## What parents should know

- CRITICAL. Caltech is need aware for international students, and you must ask for aid when you first apply. If you skip aid, or apply and do not get it in year one, you cannot ask for aid at Caltech in any later year. Decide the family budget before you apply, not after
- Older guides say that applying Restrictive Early Action (1 November) cancels aid for international students. Caltech’s current pages say REA and RD applicants can both ask for aid. Please check the rule again on Caltech’s international page before you choose your round
- Total cost for 2026-27 on campus is USD 98,622 a year (~INR 95 lakhs at ~USD 96/INR, October 2026). The average grant for the fall 2026 class was just under USD 69,000. No merit scholarships exist. Aid is need based only
- Dates for 2027 entry: REA is due 1 November 2026 and RD is due 4 January 2027. Test scores are due 30 November (REA) and 31 December 2026 (RD). Book the SAT or ACT early
- Calendar: CBSE and Cambridge A Level (March series) results both come in May, after the March decision. November 2026 A Level results come on 7 January 2027, after the RD deadline. The FP team will ask Caltech how it wants final results sent
- Caltech asks for the SAT or ACT and sets no cut-off score. Your child still needs a very strong score, mainly in Maths. Caltech’s requirement pages do not list JEE or NEET
- It is a long shot. For the class of 2029, Caltech had 11,285 applicants and a first-year class of 248. Apply to other universities as well
- Fit matters. The core is heavy in maths, physics and chemistry, and the campus is small. This is right for a child who loves those subjects, not for a broad all rounder
- US visa rules are changing. Social media accounts of the past five years must be listed and set to public. Since 15 September 2026, students are admitted for a fixed period of up to 4 years and must apply to extend. India is not on the US travel ban list, as far as we could find
- After the degree, OPT lets a student work in the US for 12 months, and STEM OPT adds 24 months. The US government says it is reviewing OPT, but we found no published rule to end it. The USD 100,000 fee on new H-1B work visas is in court and currently blocked. Check before you rely on either
