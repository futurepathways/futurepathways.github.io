## Cambridge Admission Requirements for CBSE and Cambridge A Level

> Important note for CBSE students: Cambridge accepts CBSE Class 12 only for some courses, and only at some of its Colleges. Engineering, Natural Sciences, Computer Science, Mathematics, Economics and Medicine are not on its CBSE list. For these courses, Class 12 results alone are not the route. Cambridge A Level students are on the route Cambridge knows best, but they still need the admissions tests.

## Common to both pathways

All applications go through UCAS (ucas.com), Cambridge code C05. The deadline is 15 October 2026 at 6 pm UK time. The My Cambridge Application is due by 22 October 2026 at 6 pm UK time. CBSE and Cambridge A Level results both come in May. Cambridge gives offers that depend on final results. Cambridge reads the UCAS statement and the MCA together. It cares more about love for the subject and academic ability than about clubs and activities. Please also remember the UCAS rule that a student can usually apply to only one of Oxford and Cambridge in the same year.

## Why Should Your Child Consider the University of Cambridge?

### #6 in QS, #5 in THE, and the Home of Cambridge A Levels

Cambridge is #6 in the QS World University Rankings 2027 (June 2026). It is #5 in the Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings 2027, published on 30 September 2026. In THE 2026 it was joint #3, so it moved down two places. The two lists use different methods, so please do not choose a course on a number alone. The University also has its own exam board, which sets the Cambridge AS and A Levels that TIPS students study. Many Nobel Prize winners are linked to Cambridge as students or staff.

### The Supervision System

Cambridge is known for supervisions. These are weekly sessions with a specialist in the subject, usually with one to three students. Before each one, the student writes an essay or solves a problem set. The supervisor reads it closely and then talks it through with the student. It is not a lecture. It is a close academic conversation. Every Cambridge student has this, whichever College they join. It suits a student who loves the subject and wants to be challenged.

### Natural Sciences, a Wide First Year

In Natural Sciences, students choose three science subjects plus a Maths based subject in the first year. They then narrow down later. A student who likes both Biology and Chemistry, or Physics and Chemistry, does not need to choose on day one. It is a popular course. In the 2025 cycle, Cambridge's page says there were about 5 applications for each place. Every Natural Sciences applicant must take the ESAT. Please plan early for it.

### Cambridge and India

Cambridge and India have a long shared history. India's first Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru studied at Trinity College. The mathematician Srinivasa Ramanujan from Tamil Nadu worked at Trinity with G.H. Hardy. Amartya Sen (Nobel Prize in Economics, 1998) and Dr Manmohan Singh both studied Economics at Cambridge in the 1950s. Vikram Sarabhai, who founded India's space programme, studied Natural Sciences there.

## What parents should know

- CRITICAL, test booking: Cambridge needs a test for most courses. Booking for ESAT and TMUA closed on 28 September 2026 at 6 pm UK time, UCAT closed on 16 September and LNAT on 15 September. The January sitting is only for mature Colleges. A child who has not booked should not expect to apply to these courses in this round. Please write to us today so that we can talk about 2028 entry or other UK universities. Most of them have a later UCAS deadline, 13 January 2027 at 6 pm UK time.
- CRITICAL, CBSE and the course list: Cambridge's CBSE list does not include Engineering, Natural Sciences, Computer Science, Mathematics, Economics or Medicine. A student on CBSE who wants one of these needs more than Class 12. Cambridge names A Levels and the IB. It also names five or more AP tests at score 5, with A1 in five relevant Class 12 subjects. Another route is a first year at a university outside the UK. JEE Advanced is not named on the India page, so the FP team will ask the College first. Please plan this from Grade 10.
- CRITICAL, dates: UCAS closes on 15 October 2026 at 6 pm UK time, which is 10:30 pm in India. The My Cambridge Application and transcript are due by 22 October 2026 at 6 pm UK time. Please ask your school for the transcript and predicted grades now. Missing either date ends the application.
- How the timing works for CBSE: the January offer is a conditional offer, like the one an A Level student gets. Your place is confirmed later, after your Class 12 results (about May) and any extra evidence, such as AP scores (about July). This mirrors how an A Level offer is confirmed after the May results. You do not need AP results in hand when you apply in October. The FP team will confirm with the College what it will ask for.
- University tuition for 2027 entry is GBP 30,798 to 70,554 a year (~INR 39–90 lakhs at ~GBP 127/INR, October 2026). Humanities and social sciences are GBP 30,798, Mathematics GBP 34,356, and Engineering, Computer Science and Natural Sciences GBP 46,872. Medicine and Veterinary Medicine are GBP 70,554. These figures are fixed for the whole course.
- On top of tuition, overseas students pay a College fee. It was GBP 13,356 at King's College for 2026 to 2027 (~INR 17 lakhs), and it differs by College. Cambridge estimates living costs for 2027 to 2028 at GBP 11,970 for 9 months and GBP 15,960 for 12 months (~INR 15–20 lakhs). So one year can cost about GBP 56,000 to 76,000 (~INR 71–97 lakhs) for most courses. For about Medicine and Veterinary Medicine it is about GBP 96,000 or more. Flights are extra. This is our own sum from the published figures.
- UK visa costs: the IHS is GBP 776 a year, so about GBP 2,716 for a 3 year degree (~INR 3.4 lakhs). Many Cambridge courses run 4 years, and these cost more. Cambridge is outside London. From 30 November 2026, your child must show GBP 1,203 a month for up to 9 months, which is GBP 10,827 (~INR 14 lakhs). This must sit in the bank for 28 days in a row. The Graduate Route is the UK work visa after a degree. It is 2 years if you apply on or before 31 December 2026, and 18 months from 1 January 2027. A student starting in 2027 will graduate after that date.
- Scholarships: Cambridge says few full scholarships exist for a first degree, and most help is partial and means tested. The Cambridge Trust can only be applied for after you hold a College offer. Gates Cambridge is for Master's and PhD study only. For 2027 entry, applications opened on 11 September 2026. The deadline is 8 December 2026 or 6 January 2027, depending on the course. US citizens living in the USA apply by 14 October 2026. Results come on 8 March 2027. Please do not plan a first degree on a scholarship.
- The interview matters. Most College interviews happen in the first three weeks of December. They test how your child thinks, not what they have memorised. Begin extra reading and subject talk from Grade 10 onwards.
