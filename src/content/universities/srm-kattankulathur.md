## At a glance

- **University:** SRM Institute of Science and Technology (SRMIST), formerly SRM University. It became a deemed university (one the UGC lets set its own courses and exams) under Section 3 of the UGC Act in 2002. SRM Engineering College at Kattankulathur opened in 1985.
- **Location:** Kattankulathur (KTR), Chengalpattu district, on the southern edge of Chennai. This is the main campus. SRMIST also has campuses at Ramapuram and Vadapalani in Chennai, Tiruchirappalli and Delhi NCR. SRM University AP and SRM University Sonepat are separate universities with their own admissions.
- **NAAC:** A++ in its fourth cycle, valid from June 2024 to June 2031 (NAAC grades the quality of Indian universities; A++ is its top grade).
- **NIRF 2025:** 22nd overall, 11th among universities, 14th for Engineering, 18th for Medical, 10th for Pharmacy, 25th for Research and 56th for Management. SRM Dental College (Ramapuram) is 8th for Dental. NIRF is the Government of India's own ranking. NIRF 2026 was not on nirfindia.org when this was checked.

## Which courses are most popular?

- **B.Tech** is the main draw, above all Computer Science and Engineering at KTR, which has 1,140 seats. Many other branches and specialisations are offered, such as Electronics, Mechanical, Biotechnology and Data Science.
- **Health sciences:** MBBS and BDS (through NEET), pharmacy, physiotherapy, nursing and allied health.
- **Others:** BBA, B.Com, BA, B.Sc, BCA, law (BA LLB and BBA LLB), architecture (through NATA), hotel management and agriculture.

## How do you get into B.Tech?

### CBSE and State Board students (2027-28 rules)

- At least **60 percent aggregate** in three subjects in Class 12: Physics and Mathematics (both compulsory) and one of Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology, Computer Science, Information Technology, Informatics Practices, Artificial Intelligence, Engineering Graphics, or Botany and Zoology.
- For Biotechnology, Genetic Engineering and Biomedical Engineering, PCB is also accepted. A student without Mathematics then takes an extra Mathematics course in the first year.
- A student who did not study Chemistry in Class 12 takes a bridge course in Chemistry in the first year.
- **Age:** on the India route, a B.Tech student must be at least 16 years 6 months old by 31 July of the year of the Class 12 exam. On the International route, the student must be 17 by 31 December of the year of entry (as on 18 Oct 2026). This matters for younger Cambridge candidates.

### Cambridge AS & A Level students

- **Subjects:** Physics and Mathematics at A Level are compulsory, with one of Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology, Computer Science, Information Technology, Informatics Practices or Engineering Graphics. SRM's 2027-28 page lists this route for A Level students "in any international schools within India".
- **Grades:** SRM states the 60 percent rule, but not a Cambridge grade table. Cambridge's statement of results shows a percentage uniform mark (PUM) for each AS and A Level subject, and Indian universities often use these. Ask SRM admissions which figure it will use.
- **Equivalence certificate:** SRM's international pages say a certificate from the Association of Indian Universities (AIU) may be asked for. Since 2025, the University Grants Commission (UGC) issues equivalence certificates for school results through equivalence.ugc.ac.in. Confirm with SRM which certificate it wants.

### SRMJEEE, the entrance test

- B.Tech seats go by SRMJEEE (UG) rank, followed by online counselling and choice filling. Check the exam pattern for 2027 on the SRMJEEE page.
- **Direct admission:** SRM offers direct admission (and scholarships) to first-rank students of every central and state board in India, the top 10,000 JEE rankers, top rankers in each Tamil Nadu district, and national or international sportspersons.

## How to get in: other courses

- **MBBS and BDS:** through NEET (UG), with Physics, Chemistry, Biology or Biotechnology, and English in Class 12.
- **Law:** SRM's own law test (SRMJEEL) or CLAT.
- **BBA, B.Com, BA, B.Sc and BCA:** no entrance test. Admission is on Class 12 marks, so these are good safety options to keep in the plan. SRM's India admission pages ask for 50 percent aggregate in Class 12 for BBA, B.Com, BA, B.Sc and BCA; BCA and B.Sc Computer Science also need Mathematics, Business Mathematics or Statistics (as on 18 Oct 2026). Check each course page for any extra rule.
- **Five-year law (BA LLB, BBA LLB):** 60 percent aggregate in Class 12 (as on 18 Oct 2026).

## When do applications open, and what is the fee?

- **SRMJEEE (UG) 2027:** Phase I applications close on **2 April 2027**, with the exam on 17 and 18 April 2027. Phase II applications close on **4 June 2027**, with the exam on 19 and 20 June 2027. Each phase needs a separate application.
- **Fee:** check the SRMJEEE page for the 2027 application fee.
- **International category:** application fee US$50 or ₹3,000, not refunded.

## How much does it cost a year?

- **B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering, KTR:** tuition about ₹4.75 lakh a year (SRM's programme page, checked November 2026). Fees differ by branch and campus.
- **First-year boys' hostel at KTR, 2026-27, with food:** from about ₹1.1 lakh (a 7-sharing non-AC room, off campus) to about ₹2.73 lakh (an AC 2-sharing room) a year. A common choice, a 3-sharing non-AC room with attached bathroom, was about ₹1.53 lakh. Laundry is ₹7,500 extra and optional. I did not check the girls' hostel fees, so ask SRM.
- So a first year in CSE at KTR costs roughly ₹5.9 to 7.5 lakh before books, exam fees and travel.

## Are there scholarships?

From SRM's engineering scholarships table, for the KTR campus, by SRMJEEE rank:

- **Founder's Scholarship:** 100 percent, ranks 1 to 40.
- **Merit Scholarship:** 100 percent, ranks 41 to 115.
- **Rank-based:** 75 percent for ranks 116 to 215, 50 percent for 216 to 340, and 25 percent for 341 to 490.
- The bands are different, and in places wider, at the Ramapuram, Tiruchirappalli and other campuses. Most awards cover tuition only, have limited seats, and must be renewed each year on good marks. Read SRM's scholarship policy before counting on one.
- **Board marks (Science and Humanities UG courses and BBA):** SRM's scholarship policy gives a 15 percent tuition waiver for 95 percent or more in Class 12, and 10 percent for 90 to 94.99 percent (as on 18 Oct 2026). Ask SRM how it reads Cambridge results for this; a strong PUM may help on these marks-based courses.
- **Socio-economic aid:** from the second year, for families with an income of up to ₹6 lakh a year (as on 18 Oct 2026).

## Can NRI, OCI and foreign students apply?

- For 2027-28, SRM says NRIs and PIO or OCI card holders should apply through its **International** application form, not the regular SRMJEEE route.
- Fees in this category are in US dollars. For the 2026 cycle (as on 18 Oct 2026), B.Tech Computer Science at KTR cost US$12,500 a year in the International category, or US$7,500 after the 40 percent waiver below. There was also a one-time registration fee of US$300. Fees are paid by bank transfer within 10 days of the provisional offer. Check the 2027-28 figures with SRM.
- SRM offers NRI, PIO and OCI students a 40 percent fee waiver with a SAT score or 70 percent and above in Class 12. Foreign nationals from Africa, Asia and SAARC countries get a 65 percent waiver for engineering and 50 percent for other courses. There is no scholarship in the "NRI sponsored" category (as on 18 Oct 2026).
- The international office is at admissions.ir@srmist.edu.in.

## What should families know before applying?

- **Check the campus before you pay.** One SRMJEEE rank can lead to KTR, Ramapuram, Vadapalani, Tiruchirappalli or Delhi NCR, and the fees and scholarship bands differ. SRM University AP and Sonepat are separate universities.
- **The 60 percent rule is on the three subjects, not the overall mark.** Make sure Physics, Mathematics and the third subject together reach 60 percent.
- **Plan the phase early.** Sitting Phase I in April leaves Phase II as a second chance. Cambridge students should check with SRM when final results are needed, and keep every subject in a series whose results arrive in time.
- **Use only srmist.edu.in.** SRM's public advisory says www.srmist.edu.in is its only official admissions website and admissions.india@srmist.edu.in its official admissions email, and that it uses no intermediaries.
- **SRM's pages sometimes disagree** on details such as the JEE rank limit for direct admission. Get any special offer in writing.
