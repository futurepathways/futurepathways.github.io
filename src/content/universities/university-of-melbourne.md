## At a glance

Melbourne Campus, Parkville, Victoria Walk the historic Parkville campus, close to the centre of Melbourne. This is our first Australian university in the series. Ranked #22 in the world (QS 2027) · #1 in Australia QS World University Rankings 2027: first in Australia for Academic Reputation, Employer Reputation and International Research Network.

- **University:** University of Melbourne · Member of the Group of Eight, Australia’s group of leading research universities · 9 faculties
- **Location:** Parkville, Melbourne, Victoria, Australia. The main campus sits next to the city centre. The time difference from India is about 4.5 to 5.5 hours, and direct flights run from several Indian cities (check current airlines).
- **Founded:** 1853 · Over 58,000 students · 524,000+ alumni in over 60 countries
- **City:** Melbourne is one of the larger and better known student cities in the world. Check the latest city rankings before quoting any number.
- **Popular Subjects:** Business and Economics, Science, Biomedicine, Arts, Design, Engineering and IT, Medicine, Law
- **Curriculum Accepted:** CBSE, CISCE (ISC), Indian State Boards and Cambridge A Level

A tour of the campus is available on YouTube. [Watch the University of Melbourne Campus Tour](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DHgvF1dpk_g). This video was made by an independent creator, not by the university. It shows the research buildings, the lecture spaces and student life. The university also offers a self-guided tour and a live walking tour with student ambassadors. Check the campus tour page on unimelb.edu.au for the current options.

## Melbourne Admission Requirements for CBSE/State Boards and Cambridge A Level

> Please read the Melbourne Model row and the Intake and Deadlines row with care. They are the two things most Indian families do not expect from an Australian university.

> A Planning Note Every Family Should Read First Melbourne’s year starts in late February or early March, not in September like the UK, the US or Canada. The closing date for the February 2027 start is 30 November 2026. That start is meant for students who have already finished school. A student who completes Class 12 or A Level in 2027 would normally start in February 2028. Melbourne has not yet published its 2028 dates, so treat the dates below for 2028 as likely, not fixed.

## Common to both pathways

Melbourne offers no shortcut by board. Both CBSE and Cambridge students apply through the same portal, with academic results, English proof and any course tasks. The Melbourne Model applies to everyone. A student should be happy to spend three years on a broad degree before the professional one. This is closer to the US pattern of a first degree and then professional school than to other Australian universities.

## A Melbourne Alumna Worth Knowing

Melbourne has produced researchers who changed medicine. One example is Elizabeth Blackburn, an alumna of the University of Melbourne, who shared a Nobel Prize in Medicine for work on how chromosomes protect themselves.

## Why Should Your Child Consider the University of Melbourne?

### Australia’s Top Ranked University

Melbourne is #22 in the world in the QS World University Rankings 2027 and is the top Australian university in that list. Within Australia it is first for Academic Reputation (99.7 out of 100), Employer Reputation (98.3) and International Research Network (97.4). Melbourne competes at a high level, and its entry bar is lower than Oxford, Cambridge or the Ivy League. Please remember that ranks move each year and a course is more important than a rank.

### The Melbourne Model, Broad First and Specialist Later

A student at Melbourne spends the first three years on a broad bachelor’s degree, in Arts, Science, Commerce, Biomedicine, Design or Environments. After that, the student applies for a professional degree such as Medicine, Law or Dentistry. The first year helps a student try different subjects and change direction without changing university. It suits a student who is strong in several subjects and not sure at 17 which career to choose. It is a poor fit for a student who already wants a short route to one profession, because the total time and cost are longer.

### Real Scholarships, With No Form to Fill

Melbourne gives two undergraduate scholarships by automatic consideration. There is no form and no separate closing date. For students starting in 2027 or later, the Melbourne International Undergraduate Scholarship gives 20 percent off tuition for the whole course (earlier awards gave 25 percent). It is for citizens of countries with a GDP per person of US$10,000 or less on World Bank figures, and it is based on final school results. India is expected to qualify, but Melbourne decides. The Melbourne International Excellence Scholarship also gives 20 percent off for the whole course, with at least 140 awards, to the students with the highest course entry scores. A student cannot hold either one along with another fee sponsorship or discount. Both are for students with no earlier university study. Notice that final results matter, so a student who applies with predicted results cannot be sure of the award until results arrive.

### A Large International Community

About 46 percent of Melbourne’s students are international, from over 150 nationalities. The university has an India page and an India alumni network, and it offers support for accommodation and arrival. The campus is close to the city centre. We could not confirm a recent count of Indian students, so please ask us if the number matters to your decision.

## What parents should know

- CRITICAL. Visa costs have risen quickly. The student visa is AUD 2,500 (~INR 1.7 lakhs) from 1 July 2026. The post-study work visa is AUD 4,600 (~INR 3.1 lakhs) since 1 March 2026. The two together are about AUD 7,100 (~INR 4.8 lakhs) in government fees, before health cover and any agent or test costs.
- CRITICAL. Dates differ by board. For the February 2027 start the closing date was 30 November 2026, so it is for students who finished school in 2026. A child finishing Class 12 or A Level in 2027 should plan for February 2028. Melbourne has not yet published 2028 dates, so watch its page from mid 2027.
- Tuition is high. Melbourne’s own page shows the 2026 first year fee for Biomedicine at AUD 54,780 to 61,460 (~INR 37–41 lakhs at ~AUD 67/INR, October 2026). Fees for 2027 are in Melbourne’s fee tables but we could not confirm them, and fees usually rise each year. Other degrees have their own fee, so check the course page.
- Living costs: the visa rules ask for about AUD 29,710 a year (~INR 20 lakhs) as proof of funds for the student. Real costs depend on housing and lifestyle. Melbourne also asks for a deposit and health cover when you accept an offer, so keep money ready.
- The Melbourne Model: no Medicine, Law or Dentistry as a first degree. A student does a three year bachelor’s first, then applies separately for a professional degree. Total time and cost are longer than in a direct entry system.
- National Planning Level: Australia has set 295,000 new international students for 2027, the same as 2026. It is a planning level and not a hard cap. Melbourne’s own share is 10,500 new overseas students across all courses. No university gets less than in 2026. Visa applications get priority handling until a university reaches 80 percent of its share. After that they are still accepted but processed more slowly, so apply early and do not leave the visa to the last month.
- No SAT, ACT or Indian entrance exam is asked for most courses. Some courses add selection tasks, so read each course page.
- Check English rules early. A Class 12 English mark may not replace an English test.
- Post-study work: the Temporary Graduate visa needs the applicant to be 35 or younger, and a bachelor’s graduate usually gets up to 2 years. It is a work visa, not permanent residency.
