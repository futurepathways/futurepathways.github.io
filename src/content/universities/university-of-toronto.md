## At a glance

U of T Campus: St. George, Downtown Toronto Walk the historic St. George campus. This is our first Canadian spotlight in this series.

- **QS 2027:** #32 Globally* *QS World University Rankings 2027 puts U of T at 32 and McGill at 30. In the Times Higher Education World University Rankings 2027 (published 30 September 2026), U of T is 22 and McGill is 43. Two rankings use different methods, so always check which one is quoted.
- **University:** University of Toronto (U of T) · 3 campuses: St. George (downtown, the main one), Mississauga and Scarborough
- **Location:** Toronto, Ontario, Canada. India is 9.5 hours ahead of Toronto in summer and 10.5 hours ahead in winter. The flight from India usually takes 15 to 18 hours with a change of plane.
- **Founded:** 1827, the oldest university in Ontario · Students apply to a named programme, not to the university as a whole
- **Signature Strength:** Artificial intelligence. Geoffrey Hinton, often called the "Godfather of AI", is a U of T professor emeritus, reported as a Nobel Prize winner in Physics.
- **Popular Subjects:** Computer Science and AI, Engineering, Commerce (Rotman), Life Sciences, Economics, Public Policy
- **Curriculum Accepted:** CBSE, ISC and Cambridge A Level. State Boards are looked at one by one.

## U of T Admission Requirements for CBSE/State Boards and Cambridge A Level

> Please read the rows on Standard Mathematics and the Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL) with care. A PAL is a letter from the Ontario government that most new international undergraduates need before they can apply for a study permit. Places are limited. The Mathematics rule can catch CBSE families by surprise.

## Common to both pathways

All undergraduate applications go through OUAC (ouac.on.ca). Applications are programme based. You apply to a named programme, such as Computer Science, Life Sciences or Commerce, and each programme has its own level of competition. Offers come in rounds between January and May and are usually conditional on your final results. CBSE results and Cambridge A Level results both come in May. Ask us how your final marks will reach U of T.

## U of T and Indian Students

U of T has said in public that it is proud of its Indian students and committed to their wellbeing.

One well known name from U of T is Geoffrey Hinton, often called the "Godfather of AI". He is a professor emeritus at the university and has been reported as a winner of the Nobel Prize in Physics. Computer Science students who like AI will find that a strong reason to look at U of T.

## Why Should Your Child Consider the University of Toronto?

### A Research Leader and a Home of Modern AI

U of T is 22nd in the Times Higher Education World University Rankings 2027 and 32nd in the QS World University Rankings 2027. In both lists it is among the top Canadian universities, although McGill is ahead of it in QS. Its biggest name in AI is Geoffrey Hinton. For a student who wants to study computer science, machine learning or data science, U of T is one of the most important universities in this series.

### A Work Permit After Graduation, and a Possible Route to Permanent Residence

Canada's Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) lets a graduate of a degree longer than two years work for up to 3 years. For a bachelor's degree, the student does not need to meet the field of study list that applies to college courses. The language test is the new part. Since 1 November 2024 every PGWP application needs a result at CLB 7, and the result is valid for 2 years. Do not take the test in the first year of the degree. Take it in the final year so it is still valid when you apply. A work permit can help a graduate later apply for permanent residence, but permanent residence is not guaranteed and the rules change. Please check IRCC before you plan around it.

### India and Canada Relations, and What Families Should Know

Several families have asked me about the tension between India and Canada in recent years. We want to be honest. We have no new official statement to share, and we cannot promise how relations will look in 2027. What we can say is that study permit rules are set by the Canadian government for all international students. Please read the current government advisories when your child applies.

### Programme Based Admission, a Difference Worth Understanding

Unlike the UK system (UCAS) or the single US application, U of T admits students into a named programme. Computer Science, Engineering and Commerce (Rotman) need an extra application on top of OUAC, and they are far harder to enter than the university's general figures suggest. Computer Science and Rotman Commerce need a supplementary application by 1 February 2027. Engineering needs an Online Student Profile by 15 January 2027. A strong all round profile is not enough on its own. Pick the target programme early and prepare for it.

## What parents should know

- CRITICAL, Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship: this covers tuition, books, fees and residence for four years, but a student cannot apply alone. The school must nominate its one best candidate, and each school can nominate only one student a year. For 2027 entry the school nomination closed on 9 October 2026. No the school campus put a student forward this year, so this scholarship is not open to a child now in Class 12. Families with a child in Class 11 should speak to me early in the next academic year. U of T sends the forms to schools in July, and the school must choose its nominee by early October 2027.
- CRITICAL, Mathematics: Class 12 Standard Mathematics is needed for business, engineering and science programmes. U of T says Applied Mathematics does not meet the calculus requirement. Please check your child's subject choice now.
- CRITICAL, PAL: most newly admitted undergraduates need a Provincial Attestation Letter before the study permit. It is issued after you accept the offer and pay the deposit. Places are limited, so accept and pay quickly.
- CRITICAL, PGWP language test: since 1 November 2024 you need CLB 7 on an accepted test, and the result is valid for 2 years. Take it near the end of the degree, not in the first year.
- Tuition for 2026-27: CAD 66,110 a year (~INR 44 lakhs at ~CAD 67/INR, October 2026) for first year students in Computer Science, Commerce and Arts and Science streams. Engineering is CAD 72,860 (~INR 49 lakhs). U of T's fee report lists a rise of about 4 percent a year, so fees for 2027 entry will probably be higher. Fees for Computer Science and Commerce can also change in later years.
- Other yearly costs: incidental fees of about CAD 2,100 and mandatory health insurance (UHIP) of CAD 948 for 2026-27. Residence with a meal plan was CAD 13,645 to 37,848 for 2025-26 (~INR 9.1–25 lakhs), and books cost about CAD 1,500 to 2,000. For Arts and Science streams, plan on roughly CAD 83,000 to 107,000 for the first year (~INR 56–72 lakhs). Engineering is higher.
- Study permit money proof: first year tuition plus at least CAD 23,448 for living costs (~INR 16 lakhs), for applications from 1 September 2026. The permit costs CAD 150 and biometrics CAD 85.
- Important dates for 2027 entry: apply by 7 November 2026 for first round consideration, and by 15 January 2027 at the latest. Computer Science and Rotman Commerce need a supplementary form by 1 February 2027.
- English test: IELTS Academic 6.5 with no part below 6.0. Grade 12 English alone does not give an exemption.
- State Board students are judged one by one and are not treated as equal to CBSE or ISC. Confirm your board with U of T before you apply.
