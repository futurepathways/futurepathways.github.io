## At a glance

- **Type:** A private deemed-to-be university. That is a university the UGC lets set its own courses, exams and degrees.
- **Location:** Headquarters at Ettimadai, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Other campuses include Amaravati, Amritapuri, Bengaluru, Chennai, Faridabad, Haridwar, Kochi, Mysuru and Nagercoil.
- **Accreditation:** NAAC A++, awarded in 2023. NAAC is the national body that grades universities on quality.
- **Ranking:** In NIRF 2025, the government's own ranking, Amrita is 8th among universities and 23rd for Engineering. NIRF 2026 was not on the official site when this was checked.
- **How you get in (B.Tech):** By rank in AEEE, Amrita's own entrance exam, or by JEE Main percentile.

## Popular courses

B.Tech is the main draw. Computer Science and Engineering runs at seven campuses, and Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at several campuses, with newer options such as AI with Quantum Technologies, Robotics and Cyber Security. Amrita also offers medicine, nursing, pharmacy, agriculture, law, arts, commerce and management, each with its own admission route.

## How to get in

### For CBSE and other Indian board students

1. **Eligibility.** A pass in Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. The AEEE 2027 page asks for at least 55 percent aggregate in the three and at least 50 percent in each. The B.Tech programme pages (2026 wording) say 60 percent aggregate, with 50 percent in each, and the Amaravati campus wants 60 percent in Class 10 and 12. Plan on 60 percent to be safe, and confirm with btech@amrita.edu.
2. **Age.** For the 2027 intake, date of birth on or after 1 July 2006.
3. **Entrance.** Either AEEE 2027 or a JEE Main 2027 percentile. You can register for both at the same fee. AEEE is a 150-minute computer test with 100 questions: Mathematics 40, Physics 25, Chemistry 20, Quantitative Aptitude 10 and English 5. A right answer earns 3 marks and a wrong one loses 1. Questions follow the CBSE and State Board syllabus.
4. **Selection.** After the ranks, you register for CSAP (Centralised Seat Allotment Process). You choose campus and branch there, and seats go by rank and preference. There is no management or donation quota.

### For Cambridge AS and A Level students

1. **Accepted by name.** The AEEE page lists GCE A Level (London, Cambridge or Sri Lanka) and the Cambridge HSC (Higher School Certificate) among accepted qualifications, alongside CBSE, CISCE, state boards, IB and NIOS.
2. **Same rules.** The same percentage rules apply to A Level Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.
3. **Grade conversion.** Amrita does not publish a grade-to-percentage conversion. Ask admissions how it will read your Cambridge results.
4. **Same exams.** A Cambridge student in India takes AEEE or JEE Main like everyone else. AEEE follows the CBSE and State Board syllabus, so compare it with your Cambridge syllabus early.
5. **Timing.** Cambridge March-series results came out on 19 May 2026. Keep every subject in the March series so results arrive before seat allotment.

## Application window and fee

For B.Tech 2027, applications opened on 7 November 2026. The AEEE 2027 exam window is 15 April to 7 May 2027, which Amrita calls tentative.

- AEEE only, or AEEE plus JEE: ₹1,300
- JEE Main only: ₹500
- CSAP counselling fee: ₹5,000, refunded only if none of your chosen branches is allotted

## Approximate annual cost

The fee depends on the slab you are given at CSAP, which depends on your rank, branch, seat availability and the number of applicants. Amrita says over 70 percent of seats are in scholarship slabs, and that for 2027 every admission is in the scholarship category. The 2027 fees were "to be updated" when this was checked.

For B.Tech Computer Science at Coimbatore, the 2026 tuition slabs were:

| Slab | Tuition a year, 2026 |
|---|---|
| Slab 1 | ₹1,50,000 |
| Slab 2 | ₹2,75,000 |
| Slab 3 | ₹4,00,000 |
| Slab 4 (Regular) | ₹5,25,000 |

Coimbatore hostel and mess for 2026-27: a non-AC room cost ₹52,000 to ₹68,000 a year depending on the block and sharing, and the mess ₹62,500. There is a ₹10,000 caution deposit for the hostel and another ₹10,000 for tuition, paid once.

So for B.Tech CSE at Coimbatore, a family should budget roughly **₹2.7 lakh (Slab 1) to ₹6.5 lakh (Regular slab) a year in 2026 terms**, covering tuition, a non-AC room and food, before books and travel.

## Scholarships

- **Fee slabs by rank** (above). This is the main scholarship, decided on the AEEE or JEE Main merit list. The AEEE FAQ says EWS candidates are not eligible for these slab scholarships.
- **Olympiad scholarship:** one per batch in B.Tech CSE for gold or silver medallists or national camp students in IOI, INOI, IMO or IOITC.
- **ELGi Catalyst:** full tuition for B.Tech Mechanical for able students from poor families, with study materials, accommodation, industry training and placement.

## NRI, OCI and foreign-national routes

- **NRI and OCI/PIO:** a separate registration. AEEE or JEE is not compulsory for NRI students (Indian citizens who finished Class 12 abroad) or OCI/PIO students. NRI-sponsored students studying in India must take AEEE or JEE. The application fee is USD 50 and there is a one-time registration fee of USD 300. B.Tech tuition for 2026 at Coimbatore was USD 7,500 a year for CSE and AI and Data Science, and USD 7,000 for ECE. Hostel and mess are extra, in rupees. Tuition must come from a parent's or sibling's foreign-currency account. No scholarships apply, and you cannot switch to the general category later.
- **Foreign nationals:** apply through Amrita's International Admissions office. Undergraduates must be 17 to 23 years old. Fees are in US dollars. A student with Grade 12 from outside India needs an equivalence certificate. Amrita's page still says AIU, but this is now issued by the University Grants Commission (UGC) through its equivalence portal, so confirm with Amrita. A student visa is needed (not a tourist visa), with FRRO registration within 14 days of arrival and health insurance for the whole stay.

## Things families should know

- **The rank decides the fee, not just the seat.** At Coimbatore the same CSE degree cost ₹1.5 lakh or ₹5.25 lakh a year in 2026, depending on the slab. Agree the family budget before CSAP.
- **AEEE is optional.** A strong JEE Main percentile also works. Registering for both costs the same as AEEE alone.
- **Two eligibility numbers.** Amrita's own pages say 55 or 60 percent in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. Aim for 60, with at least 50 in each.
- **Many campuses, one university.** Check the campus on your allotment. Fees and branches differ by campus.
- **No agents.** Amrita says it has appointed no agents. Never pay anyone to "reserve" a seat.
