## At a glance

- **Type:** A private, self-financing autonomous college (it sets its own syllabus and exams) affiliated to Anna University, Chennai. Its first engineering branches started in 1984.
- **Location:** Saravanampatti, on the northern edge of Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.
- **Accreditation:** NAAC A++ with a score (CGPA) of 3.62, third cycle, valid from 2 August 2022 to 1 August 2027. NAAC is the national body that grades colleges on quality.
- **Ranking:** In the 151 to 200 band for Engineering in NIRF 2025, the government's own ranking. NIRF 2026 was not on the official site when this was checked.
- **How you get in:** Through TNEA, the Tamil Nadu government's counselling on Class 12 marks, or through the college's management quota.

## Popular courses

Computer Science and Engineering is by far the largest branch, with 300 approved seats in 2024-25. Electronics and Communication (207 seats), Electrical and Electronics (138), Information Technology and Artificial Intelligence and Data Science follow. KCT also offers less common branches: Aeronautical, Automobile, Mechatronics, Biotechnology, Textile Technology and Fashion Technology.

## How to get in

### For CBSE and other Indian board students

1. **Eligibility (TNEA).** A pass in Class 12 with Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry, with an average of at least 45 percent in the three for the General category, or 40 percent for BC, BCM, MBC, DNC, SC, SCA and ST students.
2. **Nativity.** A student who studied Classes 8 to 12 in Tamil Nadu needs no nativity certificate. A Tamil Nadu native who studied outside the state needs an e-Nativity Certificate.
3. **Your rank.** TNEA builds a score out of 200 from Mathematics (100), Physics (50) and Chemistry (50). CBSE and ISC marks are normalised (adjusted) against the State Board using the top national mark in each subject.
4. **Choice filling.** List KCT and your branches in online counselling. Seats go by rank. Report to KCT with the allotment order.

### For Cambridge AS and A Level students

1. **Eligibility.** The same 45 or 40 percent rule, on A Level Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry. TNEA treats Cambridge as an "Other Board".
2. **Marks, not just grades.** TNEA says a student with grade certificates must also show actual marks. Otherwise only the lowest mark of each grade band counts.
3. **Two extra documents.** KCT's 2026-27 joining list asks for an Equivalency Certificate from students of boards other than Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Maharashtra and CBSE, and a Migration Certificate from anyone not from the Tamil Nadu State Board. Equivalence for foreign qualifications is now issued by the University Grants Commission (UGC) through its equivalence portal. Ask KCT which certificate it will accept, and apply early, because it can take weeks.
4. **Timing.** Cambridge March-series results came out on 19 May 2026, before TNEA counselling in July. Keep every subject in the March series.

## Application window and fee

For the 2026 cycle, TNEA registration ran from 3 May to 5 June 2026, the rank list came on 1 July, and general counselling ran from 20 July to 30 August. The fee was ₹500, or ₹250 for SC, SCA and ST students. The 2027 dates were not out when this was checked.

KCT also runs its own online application on the Kumaraguru Institutions portal, which in the last cycle listed interview dates in January. The fee and the steps for this route are not published, so write to the admissions office before you plan on it.

## Approximate annual cost

- **Government quota (TNEA), first year 2026-27:** ₹1,60,000 for the year, or ₹1,35,000 for a first-graduate student. KCT's sheet does not split this by branch.
- **Management quota:** the State Fee Committee tuition shown in KCT's 2024-25 disclosure is ₹1,45,000 a year for accredited branches and ₹1,40,000 for others. Other college fees are extra, so ask for the full breakup.
- **Hostel:** KCT does not publish a hostel fee on its site. The Kumaraguru Institutions hostel sheet for 2024-25 showed about ₹1,00,000 for room and mess, plus a ₹5,000 deposit for new students. Ask for the current figure.

So for a government-quota seat, budget about **₹2.6 lakh for the first year**, based on the 2026-27 college fee and the 2024-25 hostel fee, before books and travel.

## Scholarships

- **Mahatma Gandhi Merit Scholarship:** ₹10,000 a student, given to over 1,100 students a year (1,167 in 2024-25), according to KCT.
- **AICTE Tuition Fee Waiver:** for families earning under ₹8 lakh a year. KCT offered 48 waivers in 2024-25.
- **Government schemes through TNEA:** the 7.5 percent seats for government school students (fees paid by the state), the First Graduate concession, and post-matric scholarships for SC, SCA and ST students with family income under ₹2.5 lakh.

## Routes for NRI, OCI and foreign students

KCT's own disclosure says it has an NRI quota and a PIO, foreign national and CIWGC (Children of Indian Workers in Gulf Countries) quota. It does not publish who is eligible, the fees or how to apply. Write to the admissions office first. TNEA also lets OCI or PIO cardholders who are Tamil Nadu natives apply under the Open Category, unless they were born abroad after 4 March 2021. Foreign nationals will need an Indian student visa.

## Things families should know

- **No entrance exam on the TNEA route.** Class 12 Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry marks decide the rank, and Mathematics counts for half.
- **The fee is more than the tuition.** The State Fee Committee tuition for a government seat is ₹55,000, but KCT's 2026-27 first-year fee is ₹1,60,000. The rest is other college fees. Ask what it covers.
- **Bring the equivalency and migration certificates on joining day.** This applies to Cambridge and many other boards.
- **NAAC A++ runs to 1 August 2027.** Check for the renewed grade after that.
- **The management route starts early.** If you want it, ask the college in December, before TNEA opens.
