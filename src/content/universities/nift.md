## At a glance

- **Institute:** National Institute of Fashion Technology (NIFT), a statutory institute (set up by its own law, the NIFT Act 2006) under the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India.
- **Campuses:** 20 across India, including Chennai (Taramani) in Tamil Nadu and Bengaluru in Karnataka. The others are Bhopal, Bhubaneswar, Daman, Gandhinagar, Hyderabad, Jodhpur, Kangra, Kannur, Kolkata, Mumbai, New Delhi, Panchkula, Patna, Raebareli, Raipur, Shillong, Srinagar and Varanasi.
- **Degrees:** four-year Bachelor of Design (B.Des) and Bachelor of Fashion Technology (B.FTech), plus master's and PhD programmes.
- **Who runs the exam:** the National Testing Agency (NTA) conducts the NIFT Entrance Examination (NIFTEE) for NIFT.

## Which courses are most popular?

- **B.Des** in Fashion Design, Fashion Communication, Textile Design, Accessory Design, Knitwear Design, Leather Design and Fashion Interiors. Not every campus offers every specialisation, so check the seat list in the prospectus.
- **B.FTech** in Apparel Production, a technology and management degree for the clothing industry.

## How do you get in?

### CBSE and State Board students

- **B.Des:** a pass in Class 12 from any recognised board, in any stream. No minimum percentage is stated.
- **B.FTech:** a pass in Class 12 with Mathematics.
- **Age:** under 24 on 1 August of the year of admission (5 years more for SC, ST and PwD candidates).

### Cambridge AS & A Level students

- NIFT names the GCE A Level (London or Cambridge) as an accepted qualification. For B.Des a pass at A Level is enough. For B.FTech the A Levels must include Mathematics.
- **Equivalence certificate:** for a school result from outside India's boards, NIFT asks for an equivalence certificate from the Association of Indian Universities (AIU) or the University Grants Commission (UGC) before admission. Since 2025, UGC issues these for school results through equivalence.ugc.ac.in.
- **Grade conversion:** NIFT has no minimum mark, so a conversion table is not needed for eligibility. Keep the Cambridge statement of results handy.

### The entrance exam (NIFTEE 2027)

- **B.Des:** a General Ability Test (GAT, on computer) and a Creative Ability Test (CAT, on paper, drawing and design). Those shortlisted then take a Situation Test, a hands-on model-making task. The final merit list counts GAT 30 percent, CAT 50 percent and Situation Test 20 percent.
- **B.FTech:** GAT only, in two sections: Section A counts 30 percent and Section B 70 percent.
- **Marking:** 1 mark for a right answer and minus 0.25 for a wrong one.
- A B.FTech applicant with Mathematics can also apply for B.Des by taking the CAT as well.
- **Test cities in Tamil Nadu (2027 list):** Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai/Theni, Salem, Tiruchirappalli, Thanjavur/Kumbakonam, Vellore and Kanyakumari/Nagercoil, plus Puducherry.
- **After the exam:** NIFT's seat allocation is online, on a Common Merit Rank and category.

## When do applications open for 2027 entry, and what is the fee?

- **Apply online** at exams.nta.nic.in/niftee from 16 October to **24 November 2026** (extended from 16 November by NTA's public notice of 14 November 2026). With a late fee of ₹5,000 extra, the final last date is **29 November 2026**; there is no way to apply after that for 2027 entry. Corrections: 30 November to 2 December 2026, only for forms already submitted.
- **Fee:** ₹2,000 for Open, Open-EWS and OBC (NCL) candidates, ₹500 for SC, ST and PwD candidates. Open, EWS and OBC (NCL) candidates applying for two programmes pay ₹3,000.
- **Exam:** 10 January 2027. Admit cards from 4 January 2027.
- **Later stages (tentative):** written result around February or March 2027, Situation Test around March or April, final result in the first week of May, seat allocation in May and June 2027.

## How much does it cost a year?

For an Indian (non-NRI) student, on NIFT's 2026-27 fee schedule (as on 18 Oct 2026). Check the Admission Prospectus 2027 for 2027-28 fees.

- **First year:** about ₹3.42 lakh. That is ₹1,91,700 in the first semester (tuition ₹1.5 lakh, library, insurance, exam fees, one-time fees and a refundable security deposit) and ₹1,50,000 in the second. The fee is the same at every campus.
- **Later years:** tuition rises each year. On that schedule it is ₹1.58 lakh a semester in 2027-28, ₹1.66 lakh in 2028-29 and ₹1.74 lakh in 2029-30.
- **Hostel and food are extra** and are set by each campus. They are not in the prospectus. As a guide, NIFT Chennai's hostel circular for 2026-27 gives these first-year figures (including the hostel fee, a ₹2,500 refundable deposit, a ₹2,000 one-time registration fee, electricity and service charges):

| Room, NIFT Chennai, first year 2026-27 | Hostel fee a year |
|---|---|
| Boys, non-AC | ₹57,700 |
| Boys, AC | ₹80,700 |
| Girls, non-AC (includes a ₹23,000 bus fee) | ₹80,700 |
| Girls, AC (includes a ₹23,000 bus fee) | ₹1,03,700 |

Mess is extra, at ₹6,600 a month paid to the canteen provider (about ₹66,000 for 10 months). The circular is an official campus document, but the copy we read is hosted on a third-party site (as on 18 Oct 2026). So at Chennai a family should budget roughly **₹4.65 lakh to ₹5.25 lakh for the first year** on 2026-27 rates, covering tuition, hostel and food, before materials and travel. Other campuses set their own hostel fees, so ask the campus you are aiming for.

## Are there scholarships?

- **SARTHAK:** NIFT's financial help for regular students from economically weaker families. It is not automatic and depends on NIFT's criteria and approval.
- **UDAAN:** support for SARTHAK-eligible students going on approved study abroad.
- National Scholarship Portal schemes (scholarships.gov.in) and the PM Vidyalaxmi education loan scheme also apply. NRI category students cannot get NIFT financial aid.

## Can NRI, OCI and foreign students apply?

- These students do **not** take NIFTEE. They apply in NIFT's Foreign National / NRI / OCI / SAARC category and are ranked on their **SAT** score.
- The SAT score must be at least 50 percent, must not have been declared before 1 January 2025, and must reach NIFT by **31 March 2027**. Online registration is open from 16 October 2026 to 31 March 2027.
- **Application fee:** ₹11,100 in the 2026 cycle, plus bank charges and GST (as on 18 Oct 2026). Check the 2027 bulletin for this year's figure.
- Seats are extra (supernumerary): 5 per programme at Bengaluru, Chennai, Gandhinagar, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai and New Delhi, and 3 at the other campuses.
- **Unfilled NRI seats:** in the 2026 cycle, NRI seats left unfilled went to Indian candidates on the Common Merit Rank as "NRI sponsored" seats, at the higher NRI fee (Admission Guidelines 2026, as on 18 Oct 2026).
- **NRI fee:** on the 2026-27 fee schedule, ₹10,19,900 for the first year at Bengaluru, Chennai, Delhi, Gandhinagar, Hyderabad, Kolkata and Mumbai, and ₹7,22,100 at the other campuses. NIFT says it rises about 5 percent a year (as on 18 Oct 2026). The full first-year fee is paid at admission.
- Applicants need working English, and the same age limit applies.

## What should families know before applying?

- **The form is due in November of Class 12.** For 2027 entry the deadline is 24 November 2026 (29 November with a late fee), months before board exams. A student who misses it waits a year.
- **B.Des is open to every stream.** Commerce and humanities students are as welcome as science students. Only B.FTech needs Mathematics.
- **Practise drawing and design thinking.** Half the B.Des merit list comes from the Creative Ability Test, so regular sketching and making things from Class 9 and 10 helps.
- **Seats are competitive and national.** Tamil Nadu students compete on the national merit list for Chennai and Bengaluru.
- **Budget for hostel and food on top of the ₹3.4 lakh first-year fee.** At Chennai that came to roughly ₹4.65 to ₹5.25 lakh in all for the first year on 2026-27 rates. Ask the campus for its current hostel circular.
