## At a glance

- **University:** SVKM's Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies (NMIMS), a deemed university (one the UGC lets set its own courses and exams). It describes itself as a "Category 1" university.
- **Campuses:** Mumbai (Vile Parle) is the main campus. Others are Navi Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad (Jadcherla), Indore, Chandigarh, Ahmedabad (Sanand) and Shirpur.
- **NAAC:** A++ with a CGPA of 3.67 (NAAC grades the quality of Indian universities; A++ is its top grade).
- **NIRF 2025:** 24th for Management, 11th for Pharmacy, 52nd among universities and 95th overall. NIRF is the Government of India's own ranking. NIRF 2026 was not on nirfindia.org when this was checked.

## Which courses are most popular?

Through NPAT: BBA, B.Com, B.Sc Finance, BBA FinTech, an integrated BBA-MBA, B.Sc Economics, BA Liberal Arts, BBA Branding and Advertising, BBA International Business (with Kingston University, London), BBA Management and Marketing, and BBA Finance. Separate NMIMS tests cover engineering and pharmacy (NMIMS-CET), law (NMIMS-LAT, or CLAT for some law courses) and mathematics and data science (NMIMS-MST).

## How do you get in?

### CBSE and State Board students

- **Minimum marks:** 50 percent aggregate in Class 12 for most NPAT courses, and 60 percent for BBA International Business.
- **All subjects count.** NMIMS takes the aggregate of every subject the student took, not the best four or five. One weak extra subject pulls the percentage down.
- **One sitting, no compartment.** All subjects must be passed in one attempt. Compartment mark sheets are not accepted.
- **Mathematics or Statistics** is needed for B.Sc Finance, BBA FinTech, B.Sc Economics and BBA Finance.
- **Age:** 25 at most (law has its own rule).

### Cambridge AS & A Level students

- NMIMS uses at least three A Level subjects to work out the aggregate percentage. Ungraded subjects are left out.
- Cambridge is the stated exception to the "one sitting" and "no ungraded or failed subject" rules.
- If final results are not out, a Cambridge student can get a provisional admission on predicted grades. The admission is confirmed when the final results arrive and meet the rule.
- **Grade conversion:** NMIMS asks for an aggregate percentage but does not publish a conversion table. Cambridge's statement of results shows a percentage uniform mark (PUM) for each AS and A Level subject, and Indian universities often use these. Ask NMIMS which figure it will accept.
- **Equivalence certificate:** NMIMS says students from international schools must submit an AIU equivalence certificate, which is compulsory. Since 2025, the University Grants Commission (UGC) issues these certificates for school results through equivalence.ugc.ac.in, so confirm with NMIMS that it accepts the UGC certificate.

### NPAT, the entrance test

- Computer-based, 100 minutes, 120 questions: 40 each in quantitative reasoning, logical reasoning and verbal reasoning. NMIMS says the questions are in the style of the SAT. There is no negative marking.
- A student gets one main attempt and up to two retakes. The highest score counts.
- **Selection:** a merit list based on the NPAT score and Class 12 marks. NMIMS sets the cut-off. It warns that it has no agents and does not give direct admission.

### The SAT route

NMIMS also takes SAT or ACT scores for its NPAT courses, on a separate merit list. Send scores to NMIMS using code 7511. The SAT-route registration fee was ₹2,000 in the 2026 cycle. For reference, the 2026 SAT-route window ran from 20 April to 31 May 2026, with the merit list on 8 June 2026.

## When do applications open, and what is the fee?

- **NPAT 2026 (for reference):** registration 28 January to 26 May 2026, tests from 10 February to 31 May 2026, merit lists in the first week of June.
- **Fee per attempt:** ₹2,000 for one school (faculty) chosen, up to ₹7,000 for six. It is not refunded.
- **NPAT 2027:** the official site was titled for 2027 but still showed 2026 dates when this was checked. Expect registration to open early in 2027, and check npat.nmims.edu.

## How much does it cost a year?

- **Tuition:** NMIMS shows only the previous year's fees "for reference", and gives the real fee in the offer letter. For BBA, the 2025 tuition was about ₹3.6 to ₹4 lakh a year at Mumbai (the NPAT site says ₹3,60,000 and the 2026 admission handout says ₹4,00,000; NMIMS's own pages differ, so check the offer letter), ₹3.25 lakh at Navi Mumbai and Bengaluru, ₹3 lakh at Chandigarh and ₹2.75 lakh at Hyderabad and Indore.
- **Some programmes cost far more:** specialised Mumbai programmes such as BBA (International Business), the Bachelor in Business Management and Marketing and BBA Finance were listed at about ₹5 lakh to ₹9.5 lakh a year on the 2025 fee list in the 2026 handout, up to more than twice the BBA fee. Check the exact fee for your programme (as on 18 Oct 2026).
- **Hostel:** compulsory at Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Indore and Shirpur (with exceptions for local residents) and optional at Mumbai, Navi Mumbai and Ahmedabad. Hostel fees are paid separately and can cost almost as much as tuition. SVKM's fee portal lists ₹2,50,000 (three sharing) to ₹3,00,000 (two sharing) for 10 months at Bengaluru, and ₹2,25,000 for 10 months at Hyderabad (Jadcherla), each plus a ₹10,000 refundable deposit (as on 18 Oct 2026). The official pages do not say whether meals are included. So BBA at Bengaluru came to about ₹6.25 lakh in the first year with a two-sharing room. Ask for the current rates.

## Are there scholarships?

I could not find a published merit scholarship for these undergraduate courses on NMIMS's official pages. Ask the admissions office. Central and state schemes on the National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in) and bank education loans apply as for any Indian degree.

## Can NRI, OCI and foreign students apply?

NMIMS's 2026 admission handouts do not describe a separate NRI or foreign national quota for these courses. Students who finished school abroad use the same NPAT or SAT route and must give the equivalence certificate. BBA International Business needs IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL 80 (or similar) if English is not the student's first language or the English mark is below 70 percent. Confirm the details with admissions.

## What should families know before applying?

- **Every subject in Class 12 counts.** Plan the subject list with the 50 percent all-subjects rule in mind.
- **Take the first NPAT attempt early.** That leaves time for both retakes.
- **The fee you see online is last year's.** Wait for the offer letter for the real figure, and add hostel for campuses where it is compulsory.
- **Mathematics is not needed for every course.** BBA, B.Com, BA Liberal Arts and BBA Branding and Advertising ask only for the 50 percent aggregate. Courses with "Finance", "FinTech" or "Economics" in the name need Mathematics or Statistics.
