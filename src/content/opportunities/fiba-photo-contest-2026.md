## At a glance

- **What it is:** the 10th FIBA Photo Contest, run by the FIBA Foundation. FIBA is the International Basketball Federation, which runs world basketball.
- **Who can enter:** everyone, of any age, nationality or country. Under-18s need a parent's or guardian's approval.
- **Cost:** free.
- **What to send:** up to five basketball photos in total, across one or more categories.
- **Deadline:** 4 December 2026, 11:59 PM (UTC+1). In India that is 4:29 AM on 5 December, so treat 4 December as your last day.
- **Prizes:** each category winner gets photo equipment worth CHF 2,000 (Swiss francs). One of the three winners becomes the "FIBA Shot of the Year" and wins a 5-day photographer trip, with travel and stay, to the FIBA Basketball World Cup 2027.

## The three categories

1. **Elite Basketball:** top-level leagues, big tournaments and international matches.
2. **Community Basketball:** basketball in the community, such as street games, school courts, local tournaments and social projects. This is the natural fit for most school students.
3. **Vintage Basketball:** older photos, taken any time up to the early 2000s. A grandparent's photo of a college match could fit here, but only if the person entering owns the rights to it (usually the person who took it).

## Rules that matter

- **Real photos only.** Images made or changed with AI are not allowed, and nor are drawings or computer-made pictures.
- **Light editing only.** You may adjust things like saturation, contrast, sharpness, colour temperature, white balance and noise. Nothing else: no removing objects, no adding things.
- **File:** JPEG, at least 1,500 pixels, with no text, logo or caption on the photo.
- **Details to add:** your name, date of birth, nationality and a short bio, plus each photo's title, year, place and a description.

## Can a school student in India enter?

Yes. The contest is open to everyone, whatever their age. Under-18s must have their parent's or legal guardian's approval before they register. The terms say that taking part counts as that approval, so parents should read the terms with their child first.

**One thing parents must know:** by entering, the photographer gives FIBA permanent rights to use the photos, including to copy, share and use them for promotion, without asking again or paying. Only send photos you are happy to share this way. If a photo shows other people's faces, especially children, think about whether they would be comfortable with that.

## How to enter

1. Read the terms on the official entry site.
2. Pick your best photos (five at most) and check each one against the editing rules.
3. Go to the "Submit your photos" page, create an account, choose a category for each photo and fill in the details.
4. Submit before 4 December 2026.

Winners are announced in mid December 2026.
