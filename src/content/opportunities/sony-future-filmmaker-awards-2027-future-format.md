## At a glance

- **What it is:** a free international short-film competition run by Sony with Creo, a film and arts organisation. There are six categories: Fiction, Non-Fiction, Animation, Immersive, Student and Future Format.
- **The category for school students:** **Future Format**. The "Student" category is for people on a film course at a college or university, so school students should not enter it.
- **The film:** 2 to 5 minutes long, credits included, made for **vertical viewing** (9:16, the shape of a phone screen held upright). Any device and any genre are allowed.
- **Cost:** free.
- **Deadline:** 7:00 AM CST on 15 December 2026. Submit by 14 December in India so the time difference cannot catch you out.
- **Prize:** USD 2,500 in cash, a camera and lens, and return flights and a hotel to attend the workshop programme and awards ceremony in the US.

## Can a school student in India enter?

Yes. The rules say the competition is open to all members of the public, apart from people linked to the organisers. No country is left out. If you are under 18, you need your parent's or legal guardian's consent. If you are shortlisted, a signed consent form must reach the organisers within 7 days, or they may pick someone else.

To travel to the ceremony, a winner needs a valid US visa. Families should keep this in mind early, because US visa waits can be long.

## What makes a good Future Format film

The organisers want a film that is **designed for vertical viewing** from the start, not a normal widescreen film turned on its side. They say no to social-media-style clips, vlogs, adverts, trailers and branded content. Think of it as a real short film that happens to be made for a phone screen.

Some tips:

- **Plan the frame.** A tall frame is good for one person, a staircase, a tree or a lift; it is hard for wide landscapes. Choose a story that suits it.
- **Keep it short and complete.** Two to five minutes is enough for one clear idea with a beginning, middle and end.
- **Use your own sound.** You must own everything in the film. Copyrighted songs or film dialogue are not allowed unless you have permission. Use your own music or properly licensed tracks.
- **English subtitles.** If the film has a lot of non-English dialogue (Tamil, Hindi or any other language), add English subtitles on screen before you submit.
- **No fully AI-made films.** The rules do not allow films made entirely with AI.

The film must have been made between 1 January 2025 and 15 December 2026, and it must be your own work. You can send one film per category, and the same film cannot go into two categories.

## How to enter

1. Read the rules and the FAQs on the official site.
2. Upload your film to a video site (a private link is fine) and keep the link working until 31 July 2027.
3. Fill in the free entry form on the "Enter 2027" page and choose **Future Format**.
4. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian to read the rules with you and give consent.

## What happens next

Up to five Future Format films are "commended" (praised by the judges), and one wins. The workshop programme is in English with no translation.
