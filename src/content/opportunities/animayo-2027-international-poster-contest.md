## At a glance

- **What it is:** a contest to design the official poster for Animayo Gran Canaria 2027, a festival of animation, visual effects and video games in Spain. The winning poster goes on the festival's banners, tickets, website and social media.
- **Who can enter:** anyone aged 16 or over, from any country. That means students in Grades 11 and 12 (and some in Grade 10 who have turned 16).
- **Cost:** free to enter.
- **Prize:** EUR 500 before tax for the winner. The jury may also give a runner-up award (an "accésit") of EUR 250 before tax.
- **Deadline:** 31 December 2026, 11:59 PM Canary Islands time. In India that is 5:29 AM on 1 January 2027. Send it well before.
- **How you enter:** online form plus email. Everything happens from home.

## Can a school student in India enter?

Yes, if you are 16 or older. The rules say people of any nationality and any country of residence can take part. There is one extra step for under-18s: the person who fills in the entry form must be an adult. If the artist is 16 or 17, a parent or legal guardian fills in the form, accepts the rules and signs over the rights for the student.

You can enter alone or as a group of up to five people. In a group, one person enters for everyone and the prize goes in that person's name.

## What you have to design

The theme is the world of ideas and creativity, with a focus on **inspiration, fantasy and technology**. Any technique is fine: drawing, photography, illustration, 3D, collage or something else, as long as it is your own original work.

Read these rules carefully, because three of them knock out many entries:

1. **Two versions of the same poster.** One vertical (50 × 70 cm) and one horizontal (100 × 50 cm), both at 300 dpi (dots per inch, a measure of print sharpness). The shapes are different, so you cannot just crop one picture. Plan both from your first sketch.
2. **Leave the bottom 15% empty of anything important.** The festival puts sponsor logos in that strip, in both versions.
3. **No generative AI.** No part of the picture, background, texture or layout can be made with AI image tools. Simple editing tools (cropping, colour correction, removing noise) are fine. Shortlisted entrants must send their layered working files, which is how the festival checks this.

Your poster must also include this text: "Animayo Gran Canaria 2027" in large letters, and "22ª Summit and International Film Festival of Animation, Visual Effects and Video Games" in smaller letters. Do **not** put the festival dates or venues on it; the festival adds those later. Do not put your name on the poster itself, and do not mention any brand.

## How to enter

1. Fill in the online entry form on the Animayo website (the "Concurso de Carteles 2027" page).
2. Email both versions of the poster, as JPG or PDF in RGB colour, to **carteles@animayo.com**, all in one email and under 2 MB in total. The subject line should say "Concurso de Carteles 2027", your name and the name of your poster.
3. In the same email, add a short note about your design, at least five lines long.

You can send up to five designs. Each must be new: not shown or entered anywhere before, and not a design you sent to an earlier Animayo contest. Keep your poster private until the finalists are announced, or you may be disqualified.

## What happens next

- **By 22 January 2027:** the jury picks at least 15 shortlisted posters and emails the artists. Shortlisted artists get only **5 days** to send editable layered files (Photoshop or Illustrator) and a version adapted to a 300 × 200 cm banner. Check your email, including the spam folder.
- **By 5 February 2027:** finalists are listed on the festival website.
- **By 19 February 2027:** the winner is announced.
- **5 to 8 May 2027:** the festival, where the finalist posters are shown.

The winner gets a free certificate. Other finalists can ask for a finalist certificate from 1 March 2027, for EUR 12.

## Things for parents to know

- The rules are in Spanish, and the Spanish version is the one that counts. The site and form are simple to follow with a browser translator.
- The winner gives the festival the right to use the winning poster worldwide for promotion. The student is still named as the artist. Finalists who do not win keep their posters; the festival may only show them to promote the contest.
- The prize is taxed under Spanish rules. A winner living outside Spain must give bank details and a tax residence certificate to be paid.
