## At a glance

- **What it is:** Yale Young Global Scholars (YYGS), a two-week summer programme for school students, held on Yale University's campus in New Haven, USA. Students live on campus and join lectures, seminars and group projects.
- **Who can apply:** students from **all countries**, aged 16 to 18 by 18 July 2027.
- **2027 sessions (on campus only):** Session I, 20 June to 2 July; Session II, 4 to 16 July; Session III, 18 to 30 July. There is no online option.
- **Deadline:** Regular Decision closes at 11:59 PM US Eastern time on **6 January 2027** (10:29 AM IST on 7 January). The Early Action round closed on 15 October 2026.
- **Cost:** about USD 7,500 for one two-week session, as listed on the tuition page for the 2027 cycle. It covers housing, meals and course materials, but not travel.
- **Help with cost:** need-based financial aid is open to students from every country and can cover up to the full tuition.

## Who can apply

The eligibility page asks that a student:

- is between **16 and 18 years old by 18 July 2027**. Yale says no exceptions can be made;
- is now in Grade 10 or Grade 11, or the international equivalent;
- will finish school in May or June 2028 or 2029 (for schools that end the year in the middle of the calendar). In India, many boards finish in March to May, so check with YYGS if your school year does not fit neatly;
- can take part in a demanding course taught in English.

So, in India, this suits mostly students now in Grade 10 or 11 who will be 16 by mid July 2027.

## What it costs

| Item | Amount (2027 cycle) |
|---|---|
| Application fee, Regular Decision | USD 100, not refundable |
| Tuition for one two-week session | about USD 7,500 |
| Flights and US visa | Paid by the family |

Need-based aid can cover up to the full tuition, but not travel. If your child is admitted, tuition must be paid by 15 March 2027, and it is not refundable except in extreme cases such as a visa refusal or a family medical emergency, with proof.

## How to apply

1. Read the eligibility and "required components" pages on the YYGS website.
2. Fill in the online application before 6 January 2027. Ask for financial aid at the same time if you need it.
3. Give contact details for a parent or guardian and a teacher or school official, as the application asks.
4. Wait for results by 10 March 2027, then enrol and pay by 15 March 2027 if you accept.

## Is it worth it?

YYGS is a good taste of university life in the US, and it can help a student decide whether to apply abroad. It is a summer programme, not a way into a Yale degree. Weigh the full cost, including flights and the visa, against what your child hopes to get out of it.
