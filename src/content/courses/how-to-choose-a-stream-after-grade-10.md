## What the course is about

"Stream" is the word Indian schools use for the group of subjects you study in Grades 11 and 12. At TIPS, under the Cambridge programme, you choose individual AS and A Level subjects, but families still think in terms of the three big streams:

| Stream | Usual subjects | Leads most directly to |
|---|---|---|
| Science | Physics, Chemistry, Maths and/or Biology | Engineering, medicine, pure sciences, architecture, data and AI |
| Commerce | Accounting, Business, Economics, Maths | Chartered Accountancy, finance, management, economics, law |
| Humanities | History, Psychology, Sociology, Literature, Economics | Law, psychology, journalism, civil services, design, liberal arts |

The lines are blurrier than they look. Maths with Humanities, or Economics with Science, are common and useful mixes.

## Who it suits

This post is for every Grade 9 and 10 student, and especially for anyone who feels pulled in two directions or is being told what to pick by someone else.

## Subjects needed in Grades 11 and 12

Work backwards from the courses you might want. A few hard rules, approximate and to be confirmed with your counsellor:

- **MBBS and most medical courses:** Physics, Chemistry and Biology are compulsory.
- **Engineering (BTech/BE):** Physics and Maths are compulsory; Chemistry is needed for most Indian colleges.
- **Architecture (BArch):** Maths is compulsory, plus Physics and Chemistry for NATA/JEE Paper 2.
- **Chartered Accountancy, BCom, BBA, economics:** no fixed subjects, but Accounting, Economics and Maths help a lot.
- **Law (BA LLB, BBA LLB):** any stream. Good reading and reasoning matter more than subjects.
- **Design (BDes):** any stream. A portfolio matters more.
- **Psychology:** any stream, though Biology or Psychology at A Level helps.
- **Studying abroad:** most universities accept any stream but ask for specific subjects for specific courses (for example Maths for economics or computer science).

If in doubt, **keep Maths**. It is the subject most often required later and the hardest to add back.

## Entrance tests and admission routes

Your stream decides which entrance tests you can write:

- Science (PCM): JEE Main and Advanced, BITSAT, state engineering tests, NATA
- Science (PCB): NEET
- Commerce: CA Foundation, CUET, IPMAT, NPAT and other management tests
- Humanities: CLAT, CUET, NID/NIFT design tests, university tests for liberal arts

Cambridge AS and A Level results are accepted by Indian universities (through the Association of Indian Universities equivalence) and by universities abroad. Check our [Cambridge exams](/tags/cambridge-exams/) posts for details.

## A simple way to decide

1. **List what you enjoy**, not just what you score well in. Which classes make time pass quickly?
2. **List what you are good at.** Look at Grade 9 and 10 marks honestly, especially in Maths and Science.
3. **Write down three careers that interest you**, even vaguely. Look up what each one needs (our [Careers](/careers/) posts are a start).
4. **Find the overlap.** Which stream keeps the most of those three open?
5. **Talk to three people:** your FP counsellor, a teacher who knows you well, and someone who works in one of those careers.
6. **Decide, then commit.** No choice is perfect. Students change paths at every stage, and that is normal.

## Myths to ignore

- "Science is for toppers." No. It is for people who like science.
- "Commerce is easy." It is not. Accounting and Economics are rigorous.
- "Humanities has no jobs." Law, psychology, media, civil services, design and policy are full of Humanities graduates.
- "Pick what your cousin picked." Your cousin is not you.

## Duration and approximate cost

Grades 11 and 12 take two years whatever you choose. There is no extra fee for choosing one stream over another at TIPS. Coaching for entrance tests (JEE, NEET, CLAT) is an extra family cost and is a separate decision.
